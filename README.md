An entry for make.fun's **"Six Words, One Story"** bounty ($5 in USDC).

## The story

> She saved his letters. Burned mine.

Six words. A whole marriage — his letters treasured, mine destroyed — in a single sentence. What happened between them is left for the reader to fill in.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes the six-word story to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`):

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
cat out/entry.txt
```
